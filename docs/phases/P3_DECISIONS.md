# Phase 3 implementation decisions

The global medical firewall and canonical halt registry take precedence over conflicting phase examples. Ambiguous wording is **H5** (the P3 example calls it H4). Triage records that alias for traceability. No rule diagnoses a condition, assigns an exclusion, or feeds medical wording into scoring. An unambiguous declaration continues only as source text subject to insurer underwriting; `PROCEED` in the synthetic tests is a workflow route, never a coverage conclusion.

The 50 fictional scenarios in `tools/eval-sets/medical_ambiguity.json` exercise wording rules bundled in `services/api/app/compliance/medical_ambiguity_patterns.json`. They are a software regression set, not a clinical validation or a representative accuracy claim. New clinical/wording rules still need qualified review before live use.

An ambiguous statement stops before CrewAI starts. The broker must obtain a case/session medical reveal grant, review the original wording and record a decision and a reason of at least 30 non-padding characters. Neither generic Resume nor G0 can bypass an unresolved medical review. Review reasons stay encrypted and are hidden in ordinary responses; the audit contains decision metadata only. The UI offers recording a declaration, proceeding without inference, or seeking clarification. It deliberately makes no unilateral exclusion decision.

The two-agent sequential A1 crew runs a **local model fixture** in prototype mode. Its minimised context contains stated requirements and a record template; names, medical wording and contact details stay outside the crew. The existing opt-in LiteLLM gateway is provider/region gated and reserves both per-case and daily tokens. Production model credentials, approved hosting/region and clinical/compliance acceptance remain external launch requirements.

The case service and persisted state remain authoritative. G0 and G1 stay separate canonical gates, even though one P3 example combines sign-off and RFQ release. New DNR versions preserve server-authored provenance; broker edits cannot forge source or verification fields. The editable form saves after a 750 ms pause once a reason and valid fields are present, and disables sign-off while editing. G0 records the reviewing broker and budget confirmation against a fresh snapshot hash. These fields are a draft fact-find structure pending legal/compliance review of required wording.
